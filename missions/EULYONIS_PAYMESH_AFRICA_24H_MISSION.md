## 1. Mission Objective

Produce a decision-grade, demonstrable prototype and complete execution report for **EULYONIS PayMesh Africa™**, a pan-African financial interoperability and inclusion platform serving micro-merchants, informal workers, SMEs, cooperatives, tontines, institutions, governments and the diaspora.

The product must be a **rail-agnostic economic operating layer**, not another isolated wallet. It must connect to existing payment infrastructures rather than replace central banks, licensed payment systems or regulated financial institutions.

## 2. Product Thesis

Combine:

- multi-rail payment orchestration;
- merchant and microbusiness operating tools;
- community finance and tontines;
- digital identity, consent and trust;
- financial intelligence and explainable AI;
- offline, USSD, SMS, voice and agent-assisted access;
- white-label institutional deployment;
- country, currency, language and compliance packs.

## 8. Recommended Business Model

### Open Interoperability Core + Commercial Inclusive Services

1. **Free/Lite merchant tier** — basic sales, receipts, expense and cash tracking.
2. **Merchant/SME SaaS** — stock, invoicing, analytics, team controls and finance readiness.
3. **API and orchestration usage** — institution and enterprise fees per API call, active account or volume band.
4. **Managed white-label platform** — setup, annual licence, maintenance and country-pack fees.
5. **Connector marketplace** — certified adapters, extensions and revenue sharing.
6. **Institutional inclusion programmes** — government, NGO, development-bank and cooperative deployments.
7. **Payment economics** — transparent capped orchestration fee where permitted; do not rely solely on transaction take-rate.
8. **Partner finance revenue** — disclosed referral or revenue share from licensed credit/insurance partners.
9. **Impact and intelligence services** — privacy-preserving dashboards and programme measurement.
10. **Training and certification** — agent, merchant, partner and implementer certification.

Produce conservative unit economics, pricing hypotheses, sensitivity analysis and a three-year scenario model. Clearly separate verified facts, assumptions and targets.

## 9. Innovations to Validate

- **Economic Passport:** portable, consent-controlled proof of sales, savings, reliability and business history.
- **Smart Route Economics:** route selection using cost, speed, availability, FX, risk and customer preference.
- **Purpose-Bound Diaspora Payments:** school, health, supplier and verified business payments.
- **Merchant-to-Bankability Ladder:** cashbook → history → savings discipline → explainable readiness score → partner finance.
- **TontineTrust:** digital rules, contribution evidence, governance and controlled finance access.
- **Offline Trust Ledger:** signed capture, idempotency, delayed sync, conflict handling and anti-double-spending controls.
- **HumanEase Finance:** voice, pictograms, local languages and low-literacy workflows.
- **Explainable Financial Twin:** cashflow forecasts, alerts and recommendations without autonomous commitment.
- **Country Pack Factory:** currency, tax, language, compliance, provider and reporting configuration.
- **Agent Commerce Interface:** agents may prepare quotations, reconciliation and reports; payment execution requires explicit authorization.

- Do not claim licensing, certification, approval or production readiness.
- Do not connect to live payment systems.
- Do not store real personal or financial data.
- Do not invent API access, partnerships, evidence or regulatory approval.
- Do not use opaque autonomous credit decisions.

- Do not sacrifice security, traceability, accessibility or consumer protection for speed.

## 15. Final Success Test

A micro-merchant with a low academic background must be able to record a sale, see the economic result, receive a simulated interoperable payment and understand the next action within ten minutes.

An institution must understand the architecture, regulatory perimeter, operating model, commercial value and pilot requirements directly from the final report.
